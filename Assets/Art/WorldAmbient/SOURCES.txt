World ambience sources — CC0 1.0

OutsideFieldRecording.ogg: "AMB Outside 1" by Kresiek The Furry.
https://opengameart.org/content/amb-outside-1

GraveyardWind.wav, VillageWind.wav: project-generated low background drones.

OutdoorWind.ogg, NightInsects.ogg: SpritePilot ambient loops.
https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/sound-effects/ambient

Crow.ogg: SpritePilot "Bird" (crow) sound.
https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/sound-effects/creatures

WoodCreak.ogg: SpritePilot "Chest open" wood creak.
https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/sound-effects

Each source page labels the asset CC0. The game plays the loops at restrained
levels and schedules the crow and wood creak at varied intervals.
